The depot

Three shifts, three problems

You are the night analyst at a taxi depot. The trip feed for January 2024 has come in, and the dashboard says revenue is up.

Before the morning report, you check the feed. There are three shifts and three problems. Each floor is one problem.

Every floor ends on one habit: say the row count before and after, and make them reconcile.

Each floor has slides at three levels. Lv 1 gives the big idea, Lv 2 shows how the check works, Lv 3 shows the real SQL. Start at Lv 1, and switch to Lv 3 when you want to see the code. The tutor follows the level you pick. Use the Detail level picker above to change levels.

To play: press Start floor on a card. It opens that floor's slides here and the game next to them. The Floor 1, Floor 2 and Floor 3 tabs above only show the slides.

On a phone, use the Guide, Game and Tutor tabs to choose a view. Scroll the page to reach the SQL box and results in the Game view, or the question box in the Tutor view.

Lv 2 · How the check works

Each floor gives you tables in an SQL console. The console runs DuckDB inside your browser. You write SQL that creates the tables the floor asks for. Then you press Check.

The check compares your tables with the expected result, row by row, not only the counts. Floor 2 starts from a correct floor 1 result, and floor 3 from a correct floor 2 result. So you always start a floor clean.

Lv 3 · The real SQL

The data is 7,345 yellow taxi trips from January 2024, published by the NYC TLC, and the zone lookup with 265 zones. Some dirt is real, as TLC published it. Some is planted for the game: the duplicate load, and 25 drop-off ids that are 0 or empty. Ask the tutor which is which.

SELECT count(*) FROM trips_raw;
SELECT * FROM zones LIMIT 5;
